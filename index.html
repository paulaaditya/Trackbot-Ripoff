AnyConnect™: Universal Sensor Ingestion with Zero Replacements

Keep your existing pumps, water tanks, flow meters, diesel generators, and HVAC chillers. TrackBot’s edge gateway connects non-invasively, translating any proprietary signal into secure real-time digital telemetry in under an hour.

  • Non-Invasive Retrofit: CT clamps, RS-485 taps, and dry contact relays hook directly into your current panels without voiding warranties.
  • Multi-Protocol Support: Ingests Modbus RTU/TCP, BACnet MS/TP, Analog 4-20mA, Pulse Meters, and LoRaWAN simultaneously.
  • Cellular Edge Autonomy: 4G LTE IoT backhaul with 30-day offline flash buffer ensures zero telemetry gaps during power or internet drops.

Stage 02 / Predictive Intelligence

AI Anomaly Engine: Predicting Failures Before Disruption

Raw telemetry from hundreds of connected points streams into TrackBot’s proprietary time-series AI. The system identifies microscopic bearing wear, hidden pipe leaks, and motor dry-run conditions hours or days before humans notice anything wrong.

  • Acoustic & Vibration FFT: Detects pump cavitation and motor bearing degradation 3 weeks before catastrophic seizure.
  • Borewell Water Table Protection: Dynamically calculates draw-down curves to eliminate mud suction, dry runs, and motor burnouts.
  • Softener & Water Quality AI: Automates brine regeneration based on actual hardness and TDS, eliminating dangerous manual "tasting" methods.

Stage 03 / Zero-Friction Ticketing

WhatsApp AI Helpdesk: Instant Reporting to Field Dispatch

Occupants and residents despise downloading clunky dedicated apps or resetting lost passwords. TrackBot turns WhatsApp into an intelligent, multi-lingual facilities helpdesk where issues are logged in seconds and automatically dispatched to on-duty staff.

  • 100% User Adoption: Residents report issues using the app already on their phones—WhatsApp text, photos, and voice notes.
  • Natural Language AI Triage: The bot auto-extracts location, asset, and priority, generating structured tickets without human intervention.
  • Digitized Field SOPs: Technicians receive step-by-step Standard Operating Procedures on mobile with GPS verification and mandatory photo proof.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We’ve Got Answers

Everything you need to know about installing, retrofitting, and managing TrackBot.

Do we need to replace our existing pumps, meters, or valves?
No! TrackBot is engineered with our proprietary AnyConnect IoT technology that retrofits onto your current equipment regardless of brand or age. We integrate with existing water meters, sumps, borewells, and HVAC panels with zero operational downtime.
How does WhatsApp ticketing work without installing a resident app?
Residents simply message a dedicated WhatsApp business number for their society or campus. Our AI chatbot collects the apartment/office number, issue details, and photo proof, then automatically dispatches the job to the on-duty technician with zero app downloads.
How fast is the deployment and onboarding process?
Typical onboarding takes 48 to 72 hours. Our certified field engineers set up gateway sensors, calibrate level thresholds, train your on-site facility staff, and hand over the live dashboard.
Can TrackBot manage multiple building complexes from one login?
Yes. Facility Management Agencies and multi-tower corporate developers use TrackBot's centralized portfolio hub to switch between properties, compare energy/water benchmarks, and coordinate roving technician teams across an entire city.
